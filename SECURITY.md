# Security Policy

Tailnet Home is meant to be served **only inside your tailnet** (via `tailscale serve`, never `tailscale funnel`). The generated `data.json` lists your device names, Tailscale IPs and services — don't expose it to the public internet.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Use GitHub's private
[security advisory form](../../security/advisories/new) instead. I'll aim to reply within 7 days.
